🔒 Privacy by design

Sees (only when you ⌘-click): the element's label, nearby text, and URL path.
  • Never form values, passwords, or what you type.
  • Never URL tokens (query strings, OAuth codes).
  • No training — Claude API doesn't learn from your data.
  • No accounts — nothing to sign up for or sign into.
Hold ⌘ (or Ctrl on Windows) — the page dims and a spotlight follows your cursor — then click any element. OnboardAI explains what it is, and you can ask follow-ups. Esc or clicking outside closes the popup.
